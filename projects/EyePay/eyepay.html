EyePay — Gaze and Head-Gesture Payment on AR Glasses

AR glasses prototype that combines eye gaze and head gestures for hands-free mobile payment in physical and online shopping.

Role

Team Lead · Programmer, Interaction Designer, Video Producer, Presenter

Tools

Unity · C# · Vuforia · Vuzix Blade 2 · iMovie

Duration

1 week (23–28 May 2022)

Team

4 students: 3 in Singapore + 1 remote industrial designer in Germany

Specificity

Situation

EyePay was my first project with AR glasses, built in one week at the Huawei Smart Glasses Development Bootcamp organized by the NUS-HCI Lab, where more than ten teams from Singapore, China and Germany competed.

EyePay was my first project with AR glasses. It was built in one week at the 2022 Huawei Smart Glasses Development Bootcamp, organized by the NUS-HCI Lab, where more than ten teams from Singapore, China and Germany designed and built applications for smart glasses.

Our team of four combined three students working onsite in Singapore with one industrial design student joining remotely from Germany.

Task

As team leader, I owned the interaction design, the proof-of-concept app, the demo video and the final presentation for a heads-up payment prototype.

We set out to make mobile payment hands-free: letting users choose a payment source and confirm a transaction with eye gaze and head gestures, for both in-store and online shopping. As team leader, I was responsible for:

  • Designing the end-to-end heads-up interaction flow
  • Building the proof-of-concept payment app on the glasses
  • Producing the demo video and delivering the final presentation

Actions

I designed the gaze-and-head-gesture payment flow, built the app in Unity with Vuforia on the Vuzix Blade 2, and produced the demo video in iMovie, learning both interaction design methods and Vuforia within the week.

  • Interaction design — Designed the heads-up flow in which eye gaze selects the payment source and a head gesture confirms the payment, after learning task analysis and other interaction design fundamentals from our German teammate in about two days
  • Prototype — Built the proof-of-concept payment app in Unity (C#) with Vuforia on the Vuzix Blade 2, teaching myself Vuforia AR within the week
  • Coordination — Worked with the onsite team by day, with the remote designer in the afternoons, and filmed and edited the demo video at night
  • Storytelling — Produced the demo video in iMovie and delivered the final presentation

Results

EyePay won the Golden Glasses Award for Best Creativity, which led to my subsequent RA opportunity at the NUS-HCI Lab.

The project won the Golden Glasses Award for Best Creativity among more than ten teams from Singapore, China and Germany. The presentation also led to the subsequent RA opportunity at the NUS-HCI Lab led by the hackathon organizer, Professor Zhao Shengdong.

Golden Glasses Award ceremony

Key Takeaways

The project taught me to pick up new tools and methods quickly, lead a team across time zones, and communicate a prototype through video and presentation.

Through this project, I strengthened my ability to:

  • Rapidly learn unfamiliar tools and methods, from Vuforia AR to task analysis
  • Lead a distributed team across time zones under a one-week deadline
  • Communicate an interaction concept through a demo video and live presentation
